## 📌 Project Overview

The **Hospital Emergency Room Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze hospital emergency room operations and provide actionable insights into patient flow, waiting time, admissions, satisfaction, and department referrals.

The dashboard enables healthcare administrators and management teams to monitor operational performance, identify bottlenecks, and make informed decisions to improve patient care and resource utilization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Monitor daily emergency room patient visits
- Analyze patient waiting times
- Measure patient satisfaction
- Evaluate admission and discharge rates
- Identify department referral patterns
- Improve operational efficiency using data-driven insights

# 💡 Key Insights

- Approximately **52%** of patients were admitted, while **48%** were discharged.
- Most patients were attended on time, indicating good emergency room efficiency.
- The **0–9 years** age group recorded the highest number of ER visits.
- Patients requiring **General Practice** referrals represented the largest referral category.
- The average patient satisfaction score remained above **4.5/5**, reflecting positive service quality.
- Monitoring wait times helps identify peak hours and improve staffing decisions.
